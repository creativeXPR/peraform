Made of paper. Edged with the spectrum. Moves when you make something.

One material for every Quality Tools app: warm paper on a soft desk, with the approved brand spectrum running along each sheet’s cut edge. Motion belongs to the people making things. The people answering a form or sitting a test get a page that stays still.
